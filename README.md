# Signals, Stories, and Systems

## A short guide to persuasion, branding, design, and AI-assisted work

This mini textbook is for first-year students who want to understand how ideas become messages, brands, visual experiences, and responsible technical decisions. It starts with a plain white T-shirt and follows it through persuasion, archetypes, design history, product positioning, and AI-assisted creative work.

The chapters are meant to be read in order, but each can also support a studio discussion, a writing exercise, or a design critique.
